Aurora Studio

Aurora Studio is an independent game development studio based in Hong Kong. The studio develops simulation games, narrative experiences and experimental game technology inspired by places and cultures across Asia.

Its projects include Project Numazu, Revenant of Tsushima, Railway Depot: Operations & Empire and Aurora Engine.

Founder & Creative Director: Shiko Leung.

Project Numazu

An OMSI 2 add-on recreating Numazu, Shizuoka through Japanese bus routes, urban streets and coastal scenery. Released in March 2026.

Revenant of Tsushima

An original first-person Japanese folk horror game in development with Unreal Engine 5, starring Tsushima Mewa.

Railway Depot: Operations & Empire

A railway operations and management simulation about depot work, maintenance, staff and dispatch.

Aurora Engine

An experimental C++ and Unreal framework exploring NPC perception, knowledge, memory, information propagation and decision-making.
