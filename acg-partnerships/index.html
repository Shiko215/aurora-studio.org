FAQ

Frequently asked questions.

Does Aurora Studio represent any specific IP or guarantee access?

No. Aurora Studio does not represent third-party IP or guarantee licensing or talent access. Every collaboration depends on the rights holder’s interest, approvals, availability and agreed commercial terms.

Who can work with Aurora Studio?

We welcome enquiries from malls and venues, consumer brands, tourism and cultural teams, game publishers and entertainment companies with a defined audience and campaign objective.

What does the service include?

Depending on the brief, we can support audience and IP fit, partnership proposals, appropriate rights-holder outreach, local activation planning, localization, and creator and media coordination.

When would you contact a rights holder?

We first agree the objective, budget range, scope and deliverables with the client. Any outreach to a rights holder or production partner follows a defined brief.

What should we include in an initial enquiry?

Tell us the target audience, proposed location, campaign window, indicative budget and desired outcome. You may also share a preferred IP or initial concept.
