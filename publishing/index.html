BEYOND THE BUILD

From indie prototype to a clearer commercial plan.

Aurora Studio is a Hong Kong-based independent developer. Our commercial release experience includes Project Numazu, which was released in cooperation with an external publisher. We're now exploring selective publishing and co-marketing partnerships with other independent creators.

01 / RELEASE

Storefront & launch

Steam positioning, release planning, copy and launch assets, scoped to each project.

02 / GLOBAL

Localization & regions

Explore localization and market fit for Japan, Hong Kong, Taiwan and broader international audiences.

03 / AWARENESS

Creators & press

Evaluate creator outreach, media materials and practical campaign coordination.

SCOUTING / REVIEW PROCESS

What happens after you pitch?

  1. Submit a short overview, public links and, if available, a demo or gameplay video.
  2. Review project fit, development status and the kind of help you need.
  3. Discuss scope, budgets, rights and next steps only if there's mutual interest.

No funding, publishing agreement, outreach or acceptance is guaranteed by submitting a pitch.

OPEN FOR INITIAL ENQUIRIES

Submit Your Game

We welcome playable prototypes, itch.io projects, Steam pages and short pitch decks. Please include:

  • Developer or studio name
  • Contact email and region
  • Demo, gameplay or trailer URL
  • Development stage & target launch window
  • Help requested (publishing, PR, localization, etc.)
