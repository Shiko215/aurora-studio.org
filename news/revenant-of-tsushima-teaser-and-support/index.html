Watch the Revenant of Tsushima teaser

The team shared its first teaser and a way for followers to support continued development.

A new teaser gives players a look at the tone of Revenant of Tsushima, Aurora Studio’s first-person Edo-period folk horror project. The announcement also points followers to the project’s Patreon for behind-the-scenes updates and development support.

The game is still in development. The teaser communicates a creative direction, while playable content and production details are covered separately in subsequent development updates.
