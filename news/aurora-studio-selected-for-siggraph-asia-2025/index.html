Project Numazu selected for SIGGRAPH Asia 2025 Games

Aurora Studio joined the Games Start-Ups Gallery in Hong Kong with Project Numazu.

Project Numazu was selected for the Games Start-Ups Gallery at SIGGRAPH Asia 2025 in Hong Kong. The official Games program lists both Project Numazu and Aurora Studio among its participating game start-ups.

The gallery brought the studio’s detailed recreation of Numazu into a gathering of game creators and computer graphics practitioners. SIGGRAPH Asia 2025 took place at the Hong Kong Convention and Exhibition Centre from 15 to 18 December, with the Start-Ups Gallery open from 16 to 18 December.
