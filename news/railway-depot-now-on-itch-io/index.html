PROJECT UPDATE · 2026-09-16

Railway Depot opens its itch.io development hub

A new place for pre-alpha builds, screenshots and feedback on the systems behind the railway.

Railway Depot: Operations & Empire now has a page on itch.io for development builds, screenshots and community updates. The project remains in pre-alpha: visuals, interfaces and gameplay systems can change during testing.

The current focus is on depot operations, rolling stock inspection, dispatching, staff management and the small decisions that affect the next service. Player feedback on the workflow, clarity and performance will help shape future iterations.
