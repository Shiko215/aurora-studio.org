PUBLIC TEST · 2026-09-07

Revenant of Tsushima vertical slice opens for public testing

The early playable build gives visitors a first look at exploration, Ether Sight and shrine rituals.

Aurora Studio has opened a public test of the Revenant of Tsushima vertical slice on itch.io. This limited, playable experience is an early demonstration of the game’s atmosphere and core loop, rather than a finished release.

The studio’s development log describes first-person exploration, an encounter with a restless spirit, Ether Sight investigation, sacred-light purification and shrine interactions. It also explains that players may encounter unfinished assets, animation, audio, performance or pacing.

The team is asking testers for specific feedback about exploration, the clarity of Ether Sight, the ritual loop, horror pacing and the story’s accessibility. Check the original development log for the current build and access details.
