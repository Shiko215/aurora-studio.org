Aurora Studio announces Revenant of Tsushima

An original first-person folk horror journey follows Tsushima Mewa along a haunted Tōkaidō.

Aurora Studio announced Revenant of Tsushima, an original first-person Japanese folk horror adventure. Players follow Tsushima Mewa, a shrine maiden who awakens after centuries of confinement, through forgotten roads and shrines inspired by the Edo-period Tōkaidō.

The announced gameplay direction combines Ether Sight, protective charms and purification rituals. Unlike Project Numazu’s real-world transport simulation, this project develops an original supernatural setting. Features and release scope remain subject to development.
