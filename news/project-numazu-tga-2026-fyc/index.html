Aurora Studio · Project Numazu

English繁體中文日本語

Project Numazu · For Your Consideration

Campaign announcement · 4 September 2026

Aurora Studio is putting OMSI 2 Add-on: Project Numazu forward for consideration in The Game Awards 2026 Best Sim / Strategy category. This is an independent For Your Consideration (FYC) outreach campaign, not an official nomination or selection.

Project Numazu recreates the daily streets, local bus operations, landmarks and coastal atmosphere of Numazu, Shizuoka. We invite simulation media, journalists and content creators to explore our work, and we welcome continued coverage and constructive feedback.

We thank everyone who has supported Project Numazu since its commercial launch in March 2026. Independent production work on the Uchiura and wider Izu areas continues as part of our future direction. This does not constitute a confirmed Steam update or release schedule.

For review information, please contact press@aurora-studio.org. You can also explore our project page and press and media materials.

Development and free-update clarification

Editorial clarification · added 29 September 2026

Aurora Studio has not announced the abandonment of Project Numazu. Our official statement of 10 August 2026 outlined our intention to resume long-term updates, including work on N24, 長11, 沼71, 沼72 and 沼74, as well as further Numazu-area development.

Our studio is willing to prepare further improvements and aims to make future DLC updates available free of additional charge to existing owners, subject to the applicable publishing arrangements. This is a statement of our development intention, not a promise of an approved build or fixed release date.

As described in our earlier update workflow, new builds are intended to go through QA and community feedback before Steam distribution is coordinated with our German publisher, Aerosoft. Technical readiness and publisher coordination affect when an update can be released. No release date is confirmed at present.

We appreciate specific, reproducible bug reports and constructive suggestions. Please send product feedback to support@aurora-studio.org. This clarification reflects Aurora Studio's own position, and is not a statement on behalf of the publisher.

繁體中文 · TGA 2026 推薦考慮宣傳

2026 年 9 月 4 日公告

曙光工坊 Aurora Studio 以《OMSI 2 Add-on: Project Numazu》展開 The Game Awards 2026「Best Sim / Strategy」項目的 For Your Consideration（FYC）宣傳。這是工作室自主進行的作品推介，不代表作品已獲正式提名或入圍。

我們感謝自 2026 年 3 月發售以來支持沼津地圖的玩家、媒體和創作者，歡迎大家繼續報導、試玩及提出具體建議。內浦與伊豆地區的獨立開發工作將繼續推進，但不代表 Steam 版本已有確定更新日期。

後續免費更新說明

2026 年 9 月 29 日新增說明

曙光工坊並未宣布放棄《Project Numazu》。我們在 8 月 10 日官方公告 已表達重啟長期更新的意願，列出 N24、長11、沼71、沼72、沼74 等開發方向。

工作室願意製作進一步改善內容，並希望在適用的發行安排下，為現有 DLC 玩家提供毋須額外付費的後續更新。新版本預定先經 QA 測試及玩家意見收集，再與德國發行商 Aerosoft 協調提交 Steam。目前未能確認正式更新日期，亦不代表任何特定版本已獲發行批准。

錯誤報告及改善建議歡迎寄至 support@aurora-studio.org。

今後の無料アップデートについて
