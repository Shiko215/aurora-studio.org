Revenant of

Japanese folk horror on a journey through the Tōkaidō.

A world awakened

Tsushima Mewa, a shrine maiden sealed for more than three centuries, awakens to a world of yōkai. The player travels through shrines, villages, mountain passes and the coast, using Ether Sight and rituals to face supernatural threats.

This original first-person project is in development in Unreal Engine 5. Game features and scope may change.
