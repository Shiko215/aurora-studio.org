ORIGINAL CHARACTER · REVENANT OF TSUSHIMA

MEET THE TALENT

A shrine maiden
with another life.

Tsushima Mewa is Aurora Studio’s original character and the protagonist of Revenant of Tsushima. In the game’s fiction, she is a supernatural shrine maiden awakened after more than three centuries of confinement, with an ability to perceive what others cannot.

On Mewa Ch., that perspective becomes a way into Hong Kong’s urban folklore: local stories, familiar places and virtual environments that invite viewers to look closer.

MEWA CH. · THE FORMAT

Walk into the story.

Hong Kong paranormal stories meet 3D scene reconstruction and a virtual host. Episodes use the language of games and spatial storytelling to explore the settings behind urban legends. Where a story is a rumour or a recreation, we present it as such.
