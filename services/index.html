Services.

Selected creative and technical collaboration for games, simulation and place-based interactive experiences.

From a real place to a playable world.

Our work on Project Numazu brought together location research, 3D environment production and the constraints of a working transport simulator. We apply that experience to scoped collaborations with studios and cultural partners.

01 / ENVIRONMENTS

Location-based 3D worlds

Research-led world building, streetscapes and landmarks for interactive projects. Scope and source material are agreed per project.

02 / SIMULATION

Systems and playable routes

Simulation maps, route design and experience planning grounded in how places actually work.

03 / PROTOTYPING

Game and NPC prototypes

Early Unreal Engine gameplay and NPC system experiments, with clear boundaries between working prototypes and production features.

04 / INTERNATIONAL COLLABORATION

Production coordination

Creative briefs and cross-region coordination for project partners, contributors and media activity when it fits the project.
